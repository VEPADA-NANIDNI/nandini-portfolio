About

Designing with empathy, building with structure

I'm Nandini — a UI/UX Designer and B.Tech Computer Science student at RGUKT Ongole. My process sits between two instincts: understanding how a person actually thinks through a task, and translating that into a system that holds together on screen.

At StepUpMark, I design the LetsHyre hiring platform end to end — wireframes, component systems, and the navigation logic connecting employers, candidates, and admins. I joined as an intern and moved into a part-time designer role within two months, largely because I was comfortable taking an unclear brief and researching my way to a workable structure. Alongside that, I designed a complete e-commerce experience for a freelance client, Digital Photoframes, from product discovery through checkout.

Outside design, I lead and host events on campus — I ran the central hosting committee for RGUKT's annual technical fest and anchor institutional cultural and technical forums. Different skill, same instinct: read the room, communicate clearly, keep things moving.
